# What to upload — Science Worksheet & Answer Compiler

Drop files on the **Upload** screen (drag many at once). The app stores them on the
device, so they survive reloads. Filenames must match this list — that is how the
app links a worksheet to its answer key and lights the green dot.

Dots in the app: 🟢 file is in the app · 🔴 missing · 🔵 new since your last visit ·
🟡 topic partly filled.

## Suggested order
1. **Group 1** (28 files) — the typed keys already exist, so these light up green and
   are immediately the most useful.
2. **Group 2** (45 files) — the bulk of the library.
3. **Group 3** (18 files) — export to PDF first.
4. **Groups 4 & 5** (12 books) — large files; upload over wifi, one level at a time.

Total: **101 files**.

## Two routes, both supported
- **In the app** (Upload screen) — nothing to edit, stored per device, works forever.
- **In the project** — put PDFs in `worksheets/` and add a line to
  `worksheets/manifest.json`. Use this for the shared department library.

## Replacing a worksheet next year
Upload the new PDF with the **same filename**. Everything else — topic, level, typed
answer key, recorded marks — stays attached.
